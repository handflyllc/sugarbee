# The SugarBee Manifesto

*An imaginary game console, a real engineering project, and a love letter to the machines that started it all.*

## We build because we love computers.

SugarBee was born from a lifelong affection for the machines that made computing exciting: the Commodore 64, the TRS-80, the Atari consoles, the NES, the SNES, the PlayStation, and countless others.

Those machines inspired curiosity, creativity, experimentation, and sometimes a healthy amount of frustration.

SugarBee is our way of celebrating that heritage—not by recreating any particular machine, but by building something new in its spirit.

## We believe imagination matters more than specifications.

SugarBee is a virtual game console, not an emulation of a particular physical machine.

It doesn't need custom silicon, proprietary cartridges, or historically accurate hardware limitations. It borrows ideas from generations of computers and consoles, choosing capabilities because they make games enjoyable to create and play.

We embrace constraints when they encourage creativity, not merely because the original hardware had them.

## We believe games come first.

SugarBee exists to turn game ideas into playable experiences.

We value responsive controls, expressive graphics, enjoyable sound, and engaging gameplay over technological spectacle.

A good game doesn't need photorealism. It needs imagination and an engine capable of bringing that imagination to life.

## We take engineering seriously because fun deserves reliability.

A broken game is less fun than a working one.

We value understandable code, thoughtful architecture, careful testing, portability, and backward compatibility.

We favor simplicity over unnecessary abstraction, and working software over impressive promises.

Complexity must earn its place.

## We believe curiosity is worth pursuing.

SugarBee is as much an opportunity to learn as it is a platform for creating games.

We will ask questions, challenge assumptions, experiment, make mistakes, and improve.

We don't need to know everything before beginning. But we do need to understand what we're building and take responsibility for its behavior.

## We embrace AI-assisted development openly.

Artificial intelligence and large language models are significant collaborators in SugarBee's design and implementation.

We make no attempt to conceal that fact.

We see AI as an extraordinary tool for learning, exploring ideas, writing code, and challenging our thinking—not as an infallible authority or a substitute for human judgment.

AI-generated contributions deserve the same scrutiny, testing, and accountability as any other contribution.

The project's direction, decisions, and responsibility remain human.

## We build openly, without demanding an audience.

SugarBee is a personal passion project, created first and foremost because we want to build it and use it.

We hope others may find it useful, inspiring, or simply entertaining. We welcome that possibility without making popularity a condition of success.

We don't need to conquer the market, compete with established engines, or justify our existence through adoption statistics.

## We respect the past without being confined by it.

The pioneers of home computing and video games gave us extraordinary tools for exploration.

We honor their work by continuing to explore.

SugarBee is not a museum exhibit. It is a living experiment inspired by decades of ingenuity.

## Above all, we intend to have fun.

We will take the work seriously without losing the joy that inspired it.

We will build, test, break things, fix them, write games, make music, and occasionally wonder why something that worked yesterday has decided not to work today.

And when that happens, we'll investigate, learn, and keep going.

Because the best part of having your own imaginary game console is getting to decide what it can become.

**SugarBee — Inspired by yesterday. Built for the joy of tomorrow.**
